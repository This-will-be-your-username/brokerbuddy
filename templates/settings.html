Repayments from other people

A small credit from another person (a friend paying you back for dinner, a parent for the kids' swimming) is not income: it should reduce spending in the category it relates to. Such credits are flagged as repayments, and the category you give one is the spending it offsets, so Dining & Takeaway shows what you actually spent after your friends paid their share. Until you pick a category it is held out of both income and spending. You can also flag or unflag any credit yourself on the Transactions page.

Telegram bot

Categorise from your phone. Once a day the bot picks an uncategorised merchant (a high-value or frequent one), searches for what it is, and messages you suggested categories. Reply with a number, a category name or “skip”, and it is categorised, along with other uncategorised transactions from the same merchant. It also answers /next, /status and /help. It works from behind your router: nothing needs to be opened.

  1. In Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot and paste the token it gives you below.
  2. Open a chat with your new bot and press Start. It replies with your chat ID: enter that as the recipient.

The bot only talks to the recipient; messages from anyone else are ignored. Telegram sees the messages, which include the transaction description and amount. The online lookup sends only the cleaned merchant name. The token is stored encrypted.

Backup & migration

Export everything (transactions, accounts, rules, settings and saved secrets) as one file, to back up or to move BrokerBuddy to another computer. The file includes the encryption key that protects your bank tokens, so keep it private, like a password.

Import a backup

This replaces all existing data: accounts, transactions, rules and settings (including the password and the encryption key) are overwritten by the backup, and cannot be recovered. Export first if you might need what is here now. You will be signed out; sign in with the backup's password if it has one.
